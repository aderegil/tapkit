What it does

tap-teams-message writes Microsoft Teams messages with real formatting, ready to paste: emoji, bold, colors, highlights, lists, tables and copy-friendly snippets.

It is made first for chats: one-to-one, group chats and the chat of a meeting or call. It also works for channel posts.
