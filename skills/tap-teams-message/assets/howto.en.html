Step by step

  1. Ask Claude for a Teams message and give the facts. Say where it goes, the tone and how many emoji you want, or let Claude choose.
  2. Claude builds this page right away.
  3. Data: fill in the blanks. Dates and times have pickers, and each value updates every place it appears.
  4. Images: paste, drop or choose an image in an image slot, or insert one anywhere while editing.
  5. Settings: switch emoji, header, call to action or sign-off on or off.
  6. Select Copy for Teams, click the Teams message box and press Ctrl+V (Mac: ⌘+V).

Change the wording

For another tone, a shorter version or a channel post, ask Claude in the chat. It rebuilds this page.

Save and reuse

Download keeps this page with your changes. Save recipe saves the settings and structure, so Claude can write the next message the same way.

What cannot be pasted

Live @mentions, the channel subject line, the Important or Urgent flag, GIFs, stickers, Loop components and files. Add them in Teams after you paste.

Which format for which content

When the message has…Use
One main pointA bold first line with the point and the date
Facts in two dimensionsA table (owner, task, due date)
Steps in orderA numbered list
Separate pointsA bulleted list, one emoji per line
Text people will copyA snippet: one triple-click selects it
A decision or a warningA quote block
A statusA chip: green, amber or red
Something to doA call to action with 👉 and a link
A word not to missBold, color or highlight, sparingly

Settings you can ask for

Where it goes (one-to-one, group chat, meeting chat, channel), tone (formal, friendly, festive, urgent), emoji (none, a few, many), header, call to action, sign-off and length.
